🌱 GreenPath AI

AI-Driven Environmental Intelligence & Sustainability Analytics Platform

🌍 Platform Overview

GreenPath AI is a smart environmental analysis system that uses Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, and sustainability metrics to evaluate urban and natural landscapes. By analyzing satellite or park images, the platform transforms visual data into measurable environmental intelligence for sustainable planning and green auditing.

🧠 Complete Intelligent Feature Suite

  • 🌲 AI-Based Tree Detection & Counting (YOLO Model)
  • 🌍 Tree Distribution Analysis (Region-wise Classification)
  • 🔥 Tree Density Heatmap (Blue = Low, Yellow = Medium, Red = High Density)
  • 🌱 Green Cover Estimation (Green vs Non-Green Percentage)
  • 🌿 Carbon Absorption Estimation (CO₂ Impact per Year)
  • 🌫 Visual AQI Estimation using Image Clarity & Vegetation
  • 🌡 Urban Heat Reduction Modeling
  • 🌍 Smart Zone Classification (Green / Moderate / Heat Risk Zones)
  • 🌱 Plantation Recommendation System
  • 📊 Comprehensive Environmental Impact Report

⚙️ System Workflow

  1. Upload satellite, aerial, or park imagery.
  2. AI model detects trees using object detection algorithms.
  3. System calculates vegetation density and spatial distribution.
  4. Green cover and carbon absorption are estimated.
  5. Environmental indicators are combined into an Eco Health Index.
  6. Final Environmental Report is generated for analysis and download.
