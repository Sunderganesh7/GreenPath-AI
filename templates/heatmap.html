🌡️ Tree Density Heatmap

Visual analysis of tree concentration and spatial distribution

What Does This Heatmap Represent?

The tree density heatmap visually represents where trees are concentrated in the given area. It converts individual tree detections into a continuous color-based density map.

Color Interpretation

Low Tree Density
Medium Tree Density
High Tree Density

Red regions indicate dense clusters of trees, while blue regions represent sparse or open areas.

Why Is Tree Density Heatmap Important?

  • Shows spatial distribution of trees
  • Identifies green corridors and dense vegetation zones
  • Supports urban and environmental planning
  • Helps monitor forest density and deforestation
  • Useful for smart city and sustainability analysis
