Why Green Cover Estimation Is Important

1️⃣ Environmental Monitoring
Helps track vegetation loss, deforestation, and ecological imbalance.

2️⃣ Urban Planning & Smart Cities
Identifies low-green zones for parks, plantations, and green belts.

3️⃣ Land-Use Analysis
Differentiates vegetation from roads, buildings, and open land.

4️⃣ Data-Driven Decisions
Converts visual data into accurate numerical percentages.
