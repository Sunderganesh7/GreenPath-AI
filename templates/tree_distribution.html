📊 Why Tree Distribution Analysis is Important

Tree distribution analysis helps understand how greenery is spread across different regions of an area. Instead of only knowing the total number of trees, this analysis reveals where trees are concentrated and where they are lacking.

  • 🌱 Identifies areas with insufficient tree coverage
  • 🏙️ Supports smart city and urban planning decisions
  • 🧭 Helps design shaded and eco-friendly walking paths
  • 🔥 Detects imbalance or deforestation in specific zones
  • 📍 Enables targeted tree plantation and maintenance

This insight plays a crucial role in sustainable development, environmental monitoring, and climate-resilient infrastructure planning.
