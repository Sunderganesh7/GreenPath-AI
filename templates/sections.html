🌍 About GreenPath AI

GreenPath AI is an AI-powered environmental intelligence platform designed to analyze green spaces using computer vision and deep learning. It enables tree detection, green cover estimation, and sustainable urban planning by transforming visual data into actionable environmental insights.

⚙️ How It Works

📤 Upload Image
🌲 Tree Detection
🔥 Density Heatmap
🌱 Green Cover Analysis
🧭 Optimal Path Planning

🏙️ Use Cases

🌆 Smart City Planning
🌳 Park & Campus Monitoring
🔥 Deforestation Analysis
📊 Environmental Impact Assessment

🚀 Future Scope

  • Integration with live satellite and drone imagery
  • Mobile application for field-level environmental monitoring
  • Government-level dashboards for smart city planning
  • AI-based tree species identification and classification
